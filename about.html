Policy Rules

Our gym is committed to providing a safe, clean, and motivating environment for everyone.
Members are expected to follow gym rules, respect other members and staff, and use all equipment
responsibly. Proper workout attire and suitable sports shoes are required during training sessions.
Members should follow the instructions of trainers and staff, especially when using gym equipment
or participating in fitness programs.For the safety and comfort of all members, equipment should be
returned to its proper place after use, and personal belongings should be kept secure. Members are
responsible for maintaining cleanliness and avoiding behavior that may disturb others. Membership fees
should be paid according to the gym's payment schedule, and cancellations, refunds, or membership changes
will be handled according to the gym's applicable policy. Any damage caused by improper or careless use of
equipment may be subject to appropriate charges. Members are encouraged to inform the gym staff about
any relevant physical limitations before starting a workout program. The gym reserves the right to take appropriate action
if a member repeatedly violates these rules. By using our facilities, members agree to
follow these terms and help maintain a positive, respectful, and safe fitness environment for everyone.

Our Gym Maintains

We maintain our gym with a strong focus on cleanliness, safety, and equipment quality.
All workout machines and equipment are regularly checked and maintained to ensure
a safe and comfortable experience.
Our workout areas, changing rooms, and common spaces are also cleaned regularly
so that every member can enjoy a hygienic fitness environment.

Membership Rules

Members must use their membership only for themselves and should present their valid membership details when requested.
Membership fees should be paid according to the gym's payment schedule.
